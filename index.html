RESEARCH INTERESTS / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

What I’m curious about✳

I like modelling mechanisms and interactions, especially in biology, and building things that help us ask better scientific questions.

01 /

Biological mechanisms & interactions

Language models and structure prediction models that help us understand biological systems and how their parts interact.

02 /

Structures, diffusion & control

Diffusion models for 3D protein and RNA structure prediction, and algorithms for more controlled generation.

03 /

Efficient, agentic research

Frontier agentic workflows and computational efficiency. I’m currently building an efficient deep research framework for consumer-grade hardware.

04 /

Reinforcement learning & decision-making

I enjoy experimenting with on- and off-policy RL, mostly on partially observable decision problems (POMDPs), for model alignment, action control, and pruning in agentic workflows within test-time compute budgets.

05 /

Training at scale

I love training models at scale on SLURM clusters and watching the learning curves come to life in Weights & Biases.

06 /

Science with real-world impact

Working with scientific data across biology and materials science, with a focus on high-impact research and practical applications.
